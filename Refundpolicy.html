Refund Policy

Refund Eligibility

We strive to ensure your satisfaction with our music streaming services. However, refunds are available only under certain conditions, such as billing errors or technical issues preventing access to downloaded content.

Requesting a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund, please contact our customer support team within 14 days of the transaction. Provide your account details, transaction ID, and a detailed explanation of the issue.

Processing Time

Refund requests are reviewed within 7 business days. If approved, refunds will be issued via the original payment method within 10 business days.

Exceptions

We do not offer refunds for issues caused by third-party services, misuse of the platform, or changes in user preferences. Subscription cancellations prevent future billing but do not entitle to refunds for past periods.

Contact Information

For refund requests or questions, please contact our support at support@soundwave.com.
